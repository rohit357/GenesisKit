# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include being
respectful, welcoming, and constructive. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository rather
than posting them publicly.
